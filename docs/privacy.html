Privacy Policy

Maxfield Link Overlay · Effective September 3, 2026

Overview

Maxfield Link Overlay is developed and published by XClear0. The app works offline and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data.

Plan files and local storage

The app reads a Maxfield TXT or CSV plan only after the user selects that file through Android's system document picker. Parsed link instructions, current progress, and the floating-window position are stored locally in the app's private storage. They are not sent to XClear0 or any third party.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps: used only to show the user-controlled link instruction overlay.
  • Foreground service: keeps the visible overlay available while another app is in the foreground.
  • Notifications: shows that the overlay is running and provides a stop action.

These permissions are not used to collect data. The app does not request Internet access, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or game-account credentials.

Retention and deletion

Local plan and preference data remains on the device until the user imports a replacement plan, clears the app's storage, or uninstalls the app. Cloud backup is disabled by the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from children or adults.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Privacy questions may be submitted through the project's public support tracker.

隐私政策

Maxfield Link Overlay · 生效日期：2026 年 9 月 3 日

概述

Maxfield Link Overlay 由 XClear0 开发和发布。本应用离线运行，不收集、传输、出售或共享任何个人数据或敏感用户数据。

规划文件与本地存储

只有在用户通过 Android 系统文件选择器主动选择文件后，应用才会读取 Maxfield TXT 或 CSV 规划。解析后的连线指令、当前进度和悬浮窗位置保存在应用的本地私有存储中，不会发送给 XClear0 或任何第三方。

权限用途

  • 显示在其他应用上层：仅用于显示由用户控制的连线指导悬浮窗。
  • 前台服务：用于在其他应用位于前台时继续显示可见悬浮窗。
  • 通知：用于提示悬浮窗正在运行，并提供关闭操作。

上述权限均不用于收集数据。本应用不申请网络、位置、通讯录或广告标识符权限，也不读取游戏账号凭据。

保留与删除

本地规划和偏好数据会保留在设备上，直到用户导入新的规划、清除应用数据或卸载应用。本应用已关闭云端备份。

儿童隐私

本应用并非面向儿童，不会有意收集儿童或成年人的数据。

政策变更与联系方式

如本政策发生重大变化，本页面将更新生效日期。隐私相关问题可通过项目的公开支持页面提交。
