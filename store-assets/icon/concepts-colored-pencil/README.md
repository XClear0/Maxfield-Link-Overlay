# Colored-pencil icon concepts — superseded

> These drafts mix blue and green portals in the same planning network and therefore do not follow the final faction/link rule. Use the corrected concepts in `../concepts-colored-pencil-faction-correct/` instead.

These five concepts are selection drafts and do not replace the current Android launcher icon.

- **1 — Orbital plan:** one compass and four connectable portals form a guided loop; one hostile red portal is isolated above it.
- **2 — Branching plan:** two overlapping blue/green plan triangles surround a compass; two red portals sit behind a broken barrier.
- **3 — Two-stage guidance:** four blue/green portals form a diagonal queue with two small compasses; one red portal is outside the route.
- **4 — Plan fan:** one compass fans several planned links toward five blue/green portals; two red portals remain outside the fan.
- **5 — Stacked plans:** two route loops share a central compass; one red portal is separated above both plans.

## Shared generation brief

> Create an original minimalist hand-drawn colored-pencil Android app icon for an app that guides a player step by step through multiple portal-link plans. Use authentic sharpened colored-pencil lines on full-bleed white paper: fine dry grain, narrow layered strokes, visible hand pressure, and slightly imperfect circles. Do not imitate wax crayon, pastel, marker, watercolor, paint, or smooth digital vectors. Use only red, blue, green, black, and white. Portal symbols may be red, blue, or green only. Blue and green portals are connectable. Every red portal is unavailable and must be spatially isolated; no link, arrow, path, or compass pointer may touch or terminate at a red portal. Show several planned links and one current guided link. Keep the symbol centered, sparse, readable at 48 px, and safe under circular Android masks. No words, labels, map pins, UI screenshots, third-party logos, gradients, shadows, 3D, mockups, decorative borders, or watermarks.
